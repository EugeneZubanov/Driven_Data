# On-phone test checklist

Install `app-release.apk` (allow installs from your file manager). Do the steps in order and tick each one.
If a result differs, note the step number and carry on.

**Before you start:** charger nearby, phone volume up, Do Not Disturb off (step 8 turns it on).

| # | Do this | Expect |
|---|---------|--------|
| 1 | Open Quiet Night for the first time. | Intro or Record tab, dark theme, no crash. Times on the Record tab follow your phone's 12/24-hour setting. |
| 2 | Record tab › Tonight › Microphone check › Check. Allow the microphone. Talk at your pillow. | Bar moves; "The phone hears you." in green. |
| 3 | If the "Let it run overnight" card shows: tap Open setting and allow. Also try "Steps for my phone". | Android asks to let the app run in the background; after allowing and coming back, the card is gone. |
| 4 | Tap "Try a 5-minute test run". Allow notifications. Fake a few snores (loud, low, every few seconds) for a minute. | Night view with "Test run · …of 5m"; a "Recording your night" notification counting up. After 30 s the screen dims to near-black; tap wakes it. |
| 5 | Wait for the 5 minutes to end. | Results open by themselves on a night with note "Test run"; snores show where you made them. |
| 6 | Start a normal recording (Fall-asleep delay: None). Tap Hold to stop briefly. | "Keep holding…" hint; recording continues. |
| 7 | Call the phone from another phone, talk 1–2 minutes, hang up. | During the call the night view says the microphone is in use; afterwards it says Listening again. Stop (hold 1.5 s): the night shows a stretch without sound around the call. |
| 8 | Tonight › Wake-up alarm: on, set 3 minutes from now. Start recording, turn the screen off. Leave the phone. | At that time the recording stops and saves and an alarm rings until you act. Tap Snooze 5 min: it rings again about 5 min later. Then Turn off. Repeat once with Do Not Disturb on (alarms allowed). |
| 9 | Start recording. Wait 3 minutes. Settings › Apps › Quiet Night › Force stop. Open the app again. | Record tab note: "cut short at HH:MM … kept"; Open night shows the first minutes. |
| 10 | Start recording, leave the phone untouched on the Record tab for 12 minutes. | Night view dims at 30 s; after about 10 min dimmed the screen turns off; the notification still counts up. Unlock: the dimmed night view, still recording. |
| 11 | Optional, overnight: plugged in, battery setting allowed, record a full night. | A whole night in Results in the morning; note battery % used if it was unplugged. |
| 12 | Tonight › Full-night audio: on (if the row is missing, the phone has no suitable encoder: note that and skip). Do a 12-minute recording, talking now and then. | Night saves as usual and no "Full-night audio could not be recorded" note appears on the Record tab. (Playback comes later.) Turn it off again. |
| 13 | Settings › Back up, save the file. Delete one night. Settings › Restore from that file. | The deleted night comes back with its clips. |

## What the app cannot do

- It only hears what reaches the phone: keep it within about a metre of your head, face up, not under a pillow.
- It cannot tell two snorers (or a snoring pet) apart.
- It is not a medical device and cannot diagnose sleep apnoea or anything else.
- The wake-up alarm only rings if the app is still running at that time; keep your usual alarm too.
- If the phone closes the app in the night, the recording stops there (the part before is kept).

## If something fails, send back

- Step number and what you saw (a screenshot of the screen helps).
- Phone model and Android version (Settings › About phone).
- Whether the battery setting from step 3 was allowed.
- For a crash: the time it happened.
